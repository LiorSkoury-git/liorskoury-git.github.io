01 / The foundation

One system
Connected capabilities

The Digital Twin links design elements, tasks, actors, execution states, and physical feedback. Its supporting subsystems provide the infrastructure for manufacturing management and data-driven research.

Explore the system research ↗
Subsystem 01

Task model & digital thread

Tasks connect design intent with machines, tools, execution parameters, states, and recorded feedback allowing for a complete digital thread through the different stages of the process.

Subsystem 02

Data core

A shared data layer stores and exposes task data, execution records, and application-specific information and connect the tasks to other entities in the system.

Subsystem 03

Control engine

The engine dispatches tasks, coordinates actors, monitors execution, and connects workflow services.

Subsystem 04

Virtual–physical pairs

Actor interfaces connect machines to the common task model with machine-specific communication and responses.

Subsystem 05

Messaging & services

Event exchange connects modular monitoring, analysis, prediction, and control services to the execution workflow.

Subsystem 06

Interfaces & visualisation

Design tools, monitoring interfaces, and human-facing task communication connect users to the system.

03 / Data-informed Physical AI

Learn through execution.
Improve the process.

A shared task record supports services that plan, predict, and adapt physical work. Explicit rules handle known constraints; ML models learn from fabrication histories and machine readings to inform future plans and live process adjustments.
